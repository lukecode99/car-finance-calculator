Car Finance Calculator

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 30 July 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Every calculation runs on your device. The figures you enter — prices, deposits, APRs, quotes — are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no sign-in.

On-device storage

Saved comparisons are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them. We have no copy and no way to retrieve them.

Advertising

The app shows a banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. Adverts are requested as non-personalised, which means they are selected from general context rather than from a profile of your interests or your activity in other apps.

Even for non-personalised adverts, Google receives some information in order to serve them and to prevent fraud and repetition:

  • your device's IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location is derived
  • device and app information, such as device type, operating system version and the app you are using
  • whether an advert was shown, viewed or tapped

This is handled by Google, not by us — we never see it. Google's use of that data is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Tracking across apps

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. No advertising identifier is used to build a profile of you.

Analytics

None. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in the app.

Children

The app is a financial calculator and is not directed at children.

Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
